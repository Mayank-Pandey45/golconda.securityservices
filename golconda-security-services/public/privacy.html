Privacy Policy

This page explains what information the Golconda Security Services website receives and how authorized administrators use it. The website does not send form submissions by email.

Information submitted through forms

  • Contact enquiries may include your name, email address, service interest and message.
  • Job applications may include your name, email address, role of interest and optional introduction.
  • Complaints may include your name, role, phone number, subject and complaint details.

These records are stored in the company’s Supabase database so authorized administrators can review and respond to them through the Admin Dashboard. The current site does not upload application attachments.

Employee and operational information

The private Admin Dashboard can hold employee contact information, optional date of birth, residential address, profile photograph, assignments, compensation, advances and payroll estimates. These records are intended for authorized administrators and are not part of the public website responses. Access is controlled through Supabase authentication and administrator policies.

Company profile material

Company files are private in storage by default. An administrator can explicitly approve selected material for the public Company Profile page. Approved files are served with short-lived links. Do not submit or publish employee identity documents, personal records, payroll records or confidential client material as public company content.

Hosting and service providers

The site is hosted on Vercel. Supabase provides the database, authentication and file storage. The current website does not use the planned Telegram or Google Sheets integrations. Browser sessions use Supabase Auth storage; dismissal of an announcement may be remembered for the current browsing session.

Retention and security

The current application has no automated retention schedule. Authorized administrators manage submission records; financial records are designed to preserve an audit trail. Access is restricted by administrator authentication and database policies. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
